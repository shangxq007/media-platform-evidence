Frozen behavior requires: explicit node activation selects; focus alone does not select; genuine background/empty gesture clears; modifier/group behavior and lifecycle invalidation remain fail-closed. Same-owner/scope guarantees do not prohibit legitimate explicit user clears, but this was not an empty-background-origin intent. Treating the captured stage click as such is the product defect. This finding is narrower than invalidating every Canvas test or the Selection architecture.
