BOUNDARY SEMANTICS AND LIMITS
Chrome documents that frozen pages suspend freezable tasks while already-running tasks, especially the freeze callback, can finish.[1] Therefore the report does NOT equate a freeze callback with an arbitrary future timer, nor assert it is the end of every pagehide callback or a globally quiescent process. The operative retirement notification and clean later-pagehide sample precede the subsequently observed freeze event. Freeze is a corroborating transition checkpoint; the correction does not defer product retirement into freeze. Actual cleanup is observed during the prior real pagehide dispatch, not manufactured after restoration.
The new validator requires a nonempty armed Selection and one exact document/store/lifetime, ordered complete rows, one matching trusted persisted pagehide/freeze/restoration cycle, retirement notification during departure, empty same-store state at later same-event pagehide and freeze, absent proposal/marquee, a present Canvas capture collection with no held pointer, and real BFCache restoration. Missing stores/rows/freeze/notification, wrong identity or ordinary reload do not pass. No timer fallback or vacuous all()/every() is used as the cleanup proof.
This is a qualified Chromium149.0.7827.55 path, not a cross-browser guarantee. It does not prove arbitrary task/process suspension or crash/discard behavior. Observer DOM reads, subscriptions, stack collection and extra callbacks have nonzero overhead; D1 and final lifecycle are debugger-free comparisons. This task proves the observed ordering under disclosed instrumentation, not absolute instrumentation noninterference.
