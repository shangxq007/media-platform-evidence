# How to use Buffer's calendar feature

Scheduling your posts is now more seamless than ever with our improved calendar feature. Thanks to your feedback, we've evolved our calendar to include more features such as filtering posts, changing your calendar views, and creating new posts, making content planning a breeze!

:::info
Looking for the mobile version of "[Using the Buffer calendar feature on mobile](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/using-the-calendar-feature-on-the-buffer-mobile-app-YxB7DmpyJI)"?
:::

## Navigating to the calendar and choosing your view

You can navigate to your calendar by clicking ***Calendar*** in the upper right-hand corner of your publishing dashboard.

![Buffer - Publish arrow pointing to Calendar toggle](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=eab6372e-fa6a-4895-b671-7f1891c7e830 " =2490x232")

From there, you can select your desired view by toggling between ***Week*** and ***Month,*** so you can visually plan your upcoming posts.

![Buffer - Publish calendar - toggle between Month and Week view](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=99f616d6-3d98-42b3-8951-9caa880b1db0 " =2500x582")

In the month view of Buffer, you can see up to 5,000 posts. If you are scheduling more than 5,000 posts per month, we recommend using the weekly view or filtering by social channel to see all of your posts.

## What you'll see on an empty calendar

If you have three or fewer channels connected, an empty week won't be blank. We fill it with recommended posting slots labeled ***Add a Post*** next to each channel's icon. Click one to open the composer with that channel and time already selected.

![Screenshot of suggested posting times on the Calendar](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=58635550-345a-45b3-83fc-d4117272fec5 " =2264x1146")

Each active channel gets up to three suggested slots per week, based on its Posting Goal, posting schedule, and time zone, so pausing a channel or updating its schedule changes what you see. Haven't set a Posting Goal yet? We'll still recommend times based on our research. Haven't connected any channels? Your slots reflect the services you chose when you signed up. Learn more about [setting a Posting Goal](https://support.buffer.com/article/912-setting-posting-goals-in-buffer).

:::info
📝 **Note:** This applies to accounts with three or fewer connected channels.
:::

## First day of the week preferences

Some people consider Sunday the start of the week and others prefer Monday. You can choose which day of the week appears first in the weekly view of the calendar by changing your ***Preferences.*** To do this:

1. Click your profile avatar in the bottom left of your dashboard
2. Select ***Settings*** from the dropdown menu.
3. Select ***Preferences*** on the left panel.
4. Under "Start of Week," choose either ***Sunday*** or ***Monday***

![Buffer - Preferences screen - start of the week option](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=54ffa72d-092b-4c46-b5cb-619906eda2ff " =2178x1226")

## Filtering your social channels

One of the cool new features within the calendar is the ability to view and plan your content from one or several of your social channels all at once. Filtering your posts is simple; just click on the ***All Channels*** drop-down menu in the taskbar and select or deselect social channels until you've created your desired view.

![Buffer - Publish calendar - Channels filter](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=6fbd3cd0-748c-4766-8380-35c21b7fbf43 " =2496x1270")

## Filtering by post type

If you prefer to plan your content by type, you can toggle between All Posts, Drafts, Sent Posts, Scheduled Posts, and Pending Approval posts. Just click the ***All Posts*** drop-down menu in the taskbar and select which post type you want to view.

![GIF toggling between post types in the Buffer calendar](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=7c96c6b1-64a9-40c5-a2e3-a60745f55aa4 " =1247x642")

## Filtering by tag

If you're using our Tags (formerly Campaigns) feature to organize your content, you can also toggle your content by tag. Click on the ***Tags*** dropdown menu in the taskbar and select which tag you'd like to view. All posts with that tag will appear in the calendar.

![Buffer - Publish filter by Tags in the Calendar](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=bbfc6ca9-480c-488f-a7df-e072c3325dd0 " =2577x880")

## Creating, editing and deleting posts in the calendar

Instead of navigating back to your queue, you can create, edit and delete posts right from the calendar view.

### Creating Posts

1. To get started, click the *+New Post* button located in the upper right-hand corner of the taskbar, or click on the time and date you'd like to schedule the post.

![Buffer - Publish Calendar - New Post button](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=4c475fc6-1bfc-4471-a956-34b608f4b47b " =2490x1272")

2. The composer will display and you can select your desired social channels, create your content, and schedule your post. From there, you'll see your newly created post displayed in the calendar on your specified date.

### Editing posts

1. To update the text or media file of a post, click on the post in the calendar and select the **pencil icon**. This will open a preview of your post, from which you can make the necessary changes and then hit ***Save.***
2. If you only need to reschedule a post rather than update it, you can easily move the post over to the new date and time using the drag-and-drop option.

![Buffer - Publish Calendar - arrow pointing to pencil icon to edit post](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=b89e8223-8fa1-4f01-8ddf-ab3bc1d37f91 " =2512x880")

### Deleting posts

To delete a post, hover over the post, click the three dots, and select the ***Delete*** option.

![Buffer - Calendar - Delete post option.jpg](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=4991e226-75c6-4c9d-a9ce-901f5bbf7a6b " =2492x1036")

:::info
#### Notes

* You need to have ***Full Posting Access*** to channels to be able to create posts from the calendar view.
* When creating a post from the calendar’s ***Create Post*** button, you might notice that any of the social channels that are currently filtered to show in the calendar are preselected in the composer, as well. Make sure to deselect any social channels you don’t want that post to publish to.
* **A quick note on timezones**: The calendar will autodetect your current timezone from your browser, which displays in the top taskbar for reference. If your social channel is set to a different timezone than your computer is set to, the post will be reflected in your current timezone, but it will still publish at the correct time. For instance, if my post is scheduled to publish at 5pm on my London account, but I'm in EST, I'll see that post in my calendar is set to publish at 12pm EST (which is 5pm BST, London time). There isn't a way to see the timezone each social channel is set to from the calendar itself.
:::

## FAQ

### Can I schedule posts for multiple channels at once?

Yes, you can schedule a post for multiple channels by selecting the desired channels in the composer when creating your post. Click ***Customize for each network*** to customize the caption, add hashtags, and adjust your post for each channel you're posting to.

![Buffer - Publish composer with Facebook and Instagram selected.jpg](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=5bfd8dfb-8daf-49ad-955d-83b9d964816e " =1048x1268")

### Can I slide my posts to different posting slots in my calendar?

You can reschedule posts from inside the Calendar by dragging & dropping them to the desired new time and date.

### Can I share a link to the calendar or subscribe to the calendar?

At this time, it is not possible to share your calendar with others or allow others to subscribe to it. The ability to share your content with stakeholders outside your team's organization is on our team's radar and is something we're exploring.

### Is it possible to export scheduled posts in the calendar?

Buffer does not currently support exporting scheduled posts directly. However, you can use Zapier to integrate Buffer with tools like Google Calendar or Google Sheets to automatically add scheduled posts to a shareable format. Alternatively, if you're on [our Team plan](http://buffer.com/pricing), you can invite team members to your Buffer organization with specific permissions to view the calendar. For published posts, on our paid plans, [you can export captions and performance metrics](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/using-buffer-analyze-nHvyBMdqz8) as a CSV file from the Analyze tab.

### Can I add notes to the calendar?

We are currently unable to add notes to the calendar. If you'd like to see us add support for calendar notes in the future, you can [upvote this suggestion on our feature request board](https://suggestions.buffer.com/p/add-notes-to-publishing-calendar).

### What if my calendar view is empty?

If you have three or fewer channels connected, an empty week shows recommended posting slots instead of a blank calendar. See "What you'll see on an empty calendar" above. If you were expecting your own scheduled posts and the calendar still looks off, check your filters and make sure you have the correct week or channels selected. If you're part of a Team plan, [make sure you have the correct organization selected.](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/switching-organizations-on-the-web-ThhquwGNSz)
